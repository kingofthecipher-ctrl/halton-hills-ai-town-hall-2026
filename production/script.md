One assessment of the original replies; no new candidate questions or model grading rounds.
Review scenes narrate saved summaries, with four documented editorial wording changes; no score changes.
Codex wrote the AI-generated banter as fictional studio dialogue; it is not a live exchange between the reviewing models.
All 42 scores and 252 weighted entries follow the shared integer-level rubric.
Race comparisons use the official nomination roster. Mayoral nonresponse is reported by the organiser; missing replies are ungraded.
Codex wrote the closing advice and future scenarios as a synthesis of saved reviews; these are not additional independent assessments or new scores.
Candidate excerpts are exact contiguous substrings of the preserved transcripts.
Model labels are as recorded: Claude versions user-reported, Gemini label from submission, Grok version unrecorded.
