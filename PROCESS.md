# How this was made

1. Chris Golden asked the resident question and supplied seven screenshots of replies. The same fixed screenshot order was used throughout. The record does not establish that the complete Facebook thread or every later reply was supplied.
2. A shared rubric v2, output specification and dated context packet were recorded. The complete historical prompt is published without silently removing its original “Round 1” or follow-up-request wording. The organiser subsequently specified one assessment only; generated follow-up suggestions remain historical review records and were not sent to candidates or included in the video.
3. Sol 6.1 and Astra were explicitly dispatched in fresh agent sessions with the prompt and screenshots, excluding other scores. The coordinating assistant had already seen other reviews, so it did not simply relabel its own contextual judgment as an independent Sol score.
4. Chris supplied the Sonnet, Opus, Grok and Gemini outputs from other chats/apps. The recorded procedure asked for separate reviews with the common inputs and without other grades. This repository contains the supplied texts, not a full vendor-attested export of those conversations or app settings.
5. Scores were extracted and checked separately for level format, weighted arithmetic and totals. Two non-integer Grok levels required reviewer-supplied repairs; both requests and both responses are preserved. No other weighted points were changed.
6. The six review columns were combined without averaging them. The earlier inherited-model Codex review was retained as superseded evidence, not substituted for Sol 6.1 or counted as a seventh panelist.
7. Four narrated summaries were edited to avoid overstating candidate positions. Original text, reasons and final narration remain published. These were presentation edits, not new scores.
8. Codex wrote the host script, fictional robot banter and closing synthesis from the saved reviews. Kokoro stock voices perform the presentation. Candidate excerpts are labelled and checked as exact contiguous portions of the preserved transcript.
9. Campaign/announcement portraits were researched for the presentation after grading. They were not added as new evidence to rescore replies. Race groupings use the official nomination roster. Mayoral nonresponse is reported by the organiser.
10. The final MP4 was checked for all score mappings, narration inputs, encoded audio order, subtitles, chapters, full decode and visible robot animation. The output is one complete video with subtitles and chapters embedded.

## Publication is retrospective

The repository was prepared after the scoring and video production. It preserves the available records and labels changes, but does not provide a pre-scoring registration, signed model attestation or complete chat-history export. File hashes identify the published snapshot; they cannot establish when a judgment was originally made or prove that its reasoning is fair.
