# Fairness: safeguards, evidence and limits

The goal is a consistent, inspectable comparison of the supplied replies. Fairness is a claim for readers to examine, not something a GitHub repository or a passing arithmetic check can certify.

| Safeguard recorded in the process | Evidence | Remaining limit |
|---|---|---|
| Common question, screenshots, rubric and dated context | Exact prompt, seven screenshot files, transcripts | Delivery to user-run apps and their settings are not independently attested here. |
| Same six criteria and weights for every response | Rubric and 252 criterion entries | Choice of weights and anchors is contestable and can affect results. |
| Assess the answer, not party, personality, prose style or AI enthusiasm | Explicit fairness instructions | Models can still fail to follow those instructions. |
| Reasoned skepticism and challenges to inevitable job losses can score highly | Prompt fairness rules and shared employment context | All graders are AI systems; shared incentives, training and blind spots remain possible. |
| Separate reviews without supplying other grades | Recorded fresh dispatch for Sol/Astra; user-attributed other chats | This is not statistical independence. There are two OpenAI and two Anthropic models. |
| No claim of blinding | Candidate names and fixed order visible in screenshots | Identity and order effects were not experimentally measured. |
| Distinguish omission, uncertainty and factual error | Prompt, raw reviews and editorial audit | Some reviewer interpretations and claimed fact checks overreach the evidence. |
| Check scores independently from opinion | Standalone verifier, raw tables and correction log | Correct arithmetic does not validate a subjective judgment. |
| Preserve errors and repairs | Original Grok output, two correction responses, earlier superseded Codex review | Repairs were requested after seeing results; this is disclosed rather than described as preregistered. |
| Distinguish AI-written entertainment from evidence | Original reviews versus labelled script/banter/synthesis | An edited video remains a curated presentation; inspect the full replies and reviews. |
| Missing mayoral responses are ungraded | Closing scope note | Nonresponse is organiser-reported; no reason or policy conclusion is inferred. |

## Interpret scores narrowly

- A missing detail in one reply is not proof of ignorance, bad faith or lack of a policy.
- The prompts do not demand support for AI, a particular economic policy, a particular forecast or agreement with the resident.
- Length, named tools, confidence and polished prose are not meant to earn points by themselves.
- Municipal, regional, provincial, federal and private-sector responsibilities must be distinguished.
- Reviewer spread is disagreement, not a confidence interval. No panel average is shown.
- No inter-rater calibration study, randomized ordering, blind replication or human benchmark was performed.
- A model label supplied by a user or in a pasted response is not independently verified merely because it appears here.
- These seven short replies do not cover all candidates, their complete platforms or every electoral race.

The defensible claim is that the available inputs, rules, outputs, alterations and checks are open to inspection. “Unbiased,” “objectively correct,” and “the models prove who should win” are not supported claims.
