# Reporting a correction

Please identify the file, candidate and exact passage or score cell, describe the alleged error, and provide the original screenshot or a relevant primary source. Distinguish transcription/arithmetic errors, unsupported interpretations, missing context and disagreement with a judgment.

Preserve the original evidence and the reason for any accepted change. Do not silently replace a reviewer score with a coordinator's preferred score. Scoring-format repairs require a documented reviewer choice and a recalculation; changes should remain traceable in Git history and CHANGES.md.

Corrections to public files may require updated SHA256SUMS.json entries, regenerated CSVs or a new video release. The verifier is evidence of consistency, not a decision about whether a criticism is fair. No additional candidate questionnaire or scoring round is part of this project.
